About Tala

Tala Prattoy upholds the art aspects of Durga Puja. Essence of creativity combining the tools and methods of art and art installations interpreting the formative stages of the festival. Tala Prattoy's work emphasis has always been on Durga Puja as a specific art genre where the public space is being transformed into an art venue. We are committed to make that art global. Durga Puja Art 2024

Tala Prattoy, endeavouring towards global art forever, projects this year's Durga Puja Art as celebrating the journey of a Durga Puja Artist. It thus highlights Kahan — a narration by Susanta Paul, the Artist of high repute.

Watch Art with Artist

2024 introduces a never-seen-before guided walkthrough in Tala Prattoy Durga Puja Art venue where visitors may, in groups, see the artworks through a smooth walk with a guide. Here the guide is the Artist himself. This programme is chargeable and the visitors have to buy tickets for booking the slots of different times.

Frequently Asked Questions

Tala Prattoy upholds the art aspects of Durga Puja. Essence of creativity combining the tools and methods of art and art installations interpreting the formative stages of the festival. Tala Prattoy's work emphasis has always been on Durga Puja as a specific art genre where the public space is being transformed into an art venue.

Tala Prattoy is an internationally renowned public contemporary art installation collective based in Kolkata, transforming seasonal festival architecture into site-specific museum-grade installations.

Guided walkthroughs led by Lead Artist Susanta Paul and master artisans are available through online pre-booking, offering scheduled slots for intimate sensory walkthroughs.

General public access is open around the clock during festival week. Privileged Entry and Preview Show access passes for curators and media can be requested via our contact desk.

Partners and sponsors can join our ecosystem through the 'Advertise With Us' section or by reaching out directly to our institutional team at info@talaprattoy.org.
